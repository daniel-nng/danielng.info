**Who Was It For?** A student group for a case competition.

**What Was It?** This is a concept for a website for trading sports betting futures tickets, called Fan Exchange and stylized as fanEx. Users would buy and sell tickets for various reasons, including liquidity, the opportunity to make a guaranteed profit, or even peace of mind.

**What Did I Do?**

- Collaborated with the team to outline what they wanted.
- Designed the fanEx logo.
- Developed a high-fidelity UI/UX concept in Figma.
